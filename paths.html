Prop. 19 is a conditional property-tax path, not an automatic inheritance benefit.

For the parent-child exclusion, the inherited home must qualify as the transferor's principal residence and the transferee must make it their principal residence, file the homeowners' or disabled-veterans' exemption within one year, and meet continuing-use rules. Black-swan entry risk · the staging question

Does parking proceeds in short Treasuries actually soften a crash entry?

Five entry styles, same sale proceeds, same horizon. The final column is the constant ladder yield that would have been needed to match immediate equity entry over that horizon — the reinvestment risk, priced.

Sequence test
Staging comparison across designed sequences (normalised multiples)
SequenceHorizonEquities now12-month entry24-month ladder30-month ladder36-month ladderBreak-even ladder yield

A break-even yield below zero means immediate equity entry did so badly that even a negative real return on parked cash would have beaten it. A break-even yield above anything the market offers means staging was a cost, not a hedge. Both outcomes appear above — that is the point.

Where staging wins

Early deep contractions and liquidity shocks, at short and medium horizons, because the ladder is not yet in equities when the fall happens.

Where staging loses

Long horizons in a rising market. The parked cash misses both the recovery and the compounding, and 30 months is a large fraction of a five-year window.

Where it is a wash

When the ladder yield happens to match the equity risk premium over the window. The model reports that yield per cell instead of asserting which case will occur.

Primary-residence exclusion, switched on properly

The §121 exclusion only matters if the stay is long enough to clear costs

The switch subtracts a fixed exclusion from gain before tax. It is not free money: the household must own and use the home as a main home for at least 24 months of the five years before sale, must not have excluded gain on another home in the prior two years, and must handle any depreciation from rental use.

Two traps this table exposes.

First, a one-to-three-year stay can lose money outright once selling costs and carry costs are charged, so “live in it for a while” is not automatically a good bridge. Second, the exclusion can be worth nothing in a cell where the gain is already below the exclusion threshold — in which case the whole occupancy decision rests on property tax and housing value, not on §121.

Method and limits

How the arithmetic works

Returns

Annual equity, property, rent, Treasury and inflation sequences, converted to monthly rates and compounded monthly. Beyond twenty years the designed sequence repeats; this is disclosed rather than hidden.

Property

Capital value grows with the property sequence. Rent is a yield on opening value, reduced by vacancy and an operating-cost ratio. Accumulated net cash earns the Treasury series. Depreciation is straight-line on 75% of value over 27.5 years, used only when tax is switched on.

Sale

Sale costs are deducted first. Gain is measured against inherited basis, recapture is taken at 25% (the unrecaptured §1250 maximum) and the remainder at the selected capital-gains rate. Without a valuation, basis is set equal to the starting unit.

What this page cannot do.

It cannot size the estate tax, the California marginal rate, the actual property-tax bill, the rent, the condition, the leases or the insurance. It deliberately omits the household's whole-estate position, and it assigns no probability to any sequence. Use it to choose questions for professionals, then rebuild the numbers privately from verified evidence and current law.
