Documented history beside the model

Historical anchors are context, not forecasts

The selected stress paths use transparent synthetic sequences. Real growth and inflation are never consistent over a 50-year holding period, so these uneven paths test sequence risk instead of pretending at a smooth average. The sources below establish the historical timing and conditions that motivate the tests; they do not validate the model returns.

Reinvestment risk beside the headline

A Treasury ladder is a decision tool, not a return guarantee

For the staged sale path, short bills and notes reduce immediate equity-entry exposure. They also create a known need to reinvest as securities mature. The future rate is unknown; a ladder cannot guarantee that today’s rate will be available later.

Investment-policy gate

Write the policy before proceeds arrive.

  • Set the liquidity reserve for tax, estate, repairs, and household needs.
  • Define allowable 3M–2Y Treasury maturities and custody/tax reporting.
  • Set 24–36 month hold and 12-month phase-in rules; specify what pauses or accelerates them.
  • Set equity allocation, rebalancing, concentration, and spending rules.
  • Record decision owner, date, review frequency, and conditions that override the policy.

A documented policy avoids making a sequence-risk decision during grief, a market shock, or an urgent sale process.

Methodology & limits

How to read the stress-test math

What the numbers are

Calculated nominal outcomes from a generic $1.0M start, uneven annual inputs, monthly compounding, and a staged path that parks proceeds for 24–36 months then phases stock purchases over 12 months.

What the numbers are not

They are not valuations, rent forecasts, property-tax bills, tax returns, historical reconstructions, expected returns, or individualized investment advice. No probability is assigned.

What can reverse a result

Basis, debt, tax rate area, estate size, marital/gift history, primary-residence facts, sale costs, local regulation, condition, insurance, actual market rent, vacancy, and capital work can all dominate the generic return difference.
