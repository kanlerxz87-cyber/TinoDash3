Assumption register · no hidden numbers

Every number in the model is on this page.

If a figure appears in a result and it is not an official published rate, it is listed here with the reason it was chosen and the specific private fact that should replace it. Round-number conventions are labelled as conventions, not facts.

Three labels only

Fact · Convention · Unknown

Fact is linked to an official page. Convention is a documented choice inside the model. Unknown is deliberately blank and blocks a real recommendation.

Read this before any result

What kind of number are you looking at?

Fact

Published and linked

Statutes, exclusions, rates, filing windows and published fee schedules. Each has a date and an official URL. A fact here is not a prediction that the same rule will exist in 2040–2050.

Convention

Chosen, disclosed, replaceable

Cost ratios, vacancy, rent yield and the designed return sequences. They exist to test robustness. They are not market data, not appraisals, and not estimates for this property.

Unknown

Blank on purpose

Address, parcel, rate area, valuation, rent roll, leases, insurance quotes, condition, debt, estate size, prior gifts, and occupancy intent. Publishing or inventing any of these would be a privacy failure and a modelling failure.

Not assumed anywhere

Inputs the model deliberately refuses to invent

  • Date-of-death fair market value, and therefore inherited basis.
  • Tax rate area, voter-approved debt rates, direct charges and any Mello-Roos or parcel tax.
  • Whether the transferor used the home as a principal residence, which governs Prop. 19 eligibility.
  • Whether the heir will occupy, for how long, and when.
  • Condition, deferred maintenance, roof, foundation, sewer, electrical capacity.
  • Insurance availability, deductible structure, earthquake and wildfire cover.
  • Water, power and solar-credit history.
  • Debt, liens, payoff penalties and estate liquidity needs.
  • Whole-estate value, prior taxable gifts, marital history and portability.
  • Any like-kind exchange intent.
  • Property B's leases, unit lawfulness, registration and rent-increase licence status.
Privacy rule for this page

No real figure belongs in this table.

If a real number is entered here, the repository becomes a searchable dossier. The model therefore works in multiples, and the private workbook holds the only copy of the actual amounts.

Rebuild rule

Every validated input change forces a rebuild.

After a valuation, rate-area lookup, rent opinion, insurance quote, lease review or law change, re-run the comparison and record whether the leader moved, by how much, and why. Keep that record next to the decision.
