## Irregularities for review (not resolved here)

- **A. Proposition H ballot text.** The results page text shows a "maximum of $400,000 per parcel," while the site's description of the measure's structure ("rates vary by parcel type and building area") and dates ("1 Jul 2027 through 30 Jun 2042") were **not** confirmed by this check. Check the operative measure text and the official voter pamphlet before using any Prop H date or rate in a decision.
- **B. Form year.** The FTB source checked is the **2025** Form 540 instructions. Any 2026 return will use a later instruction set. Recheck the line 62 wording and threshold when the 2026 instructions are posted.
- **C. Rolling pages.** The IRS "What's new" page is a rolling update page. The snapshot date is the review date above; a later edit can change the text without notice.
- **D. Preliminary election data.** Proposition H is not enacted by this check. It stays conditional until certification, operative text, and effective dates are verified.
