Agreed before the figures arrive

What would make the household choose each path.

These rules were written and committed on 25 Nov 2026 before any model output existed for this project, so a later projection cannot be reverse-engineered to defend a preferred outcome. Each rule states its condition, its outcome when unmet, and the official source route. Household-specific thresholds stay blank until the household sets them privately.

Change control

Changing a rule needs a dated entry.

Any later change requires a dated decision-log entry stating the reason and the owner who approved it. Thresholds are never tuned to match a projection.

R0 Evidence gate — no recommendation while gating facts are open
No path is recommended until each gating item is verified from primary records: (a) a written date-of-death valuation of Property A; (b) Property A's tax-rate area and current bill read from the assessor's record; (c) the heir's Prop. 19 facts (intent and ability to occupy as principal residence, and the one-year claim); (d) whole-estate value and the portability position; (e) written insurance quotes for both properties; (f) Property B lease, rent-registration and local ballot-measure status.		 All six items verified. Any single open item blocks ranking. HOLD — collect evidence. The model may be run, but its output is labelled "not decidable". County assessor · CA BOE Prop. 19 · IRS estate-tax FAQ · Official election results
R1 Living paths need documented Prop. 19 facts
Living-based paths (live 2 years then sell; live long term) are ranked only if the heir can document a plan to make Property A the principal residence within one year and to file the claim within the BOE deadlines. Without that documentation the living paths keep full reassessment and are shown as "excluded from ranking".		 Documented facts for all three conditions (occupancy plan, one-year move-in, claim filing). Living paths excluded from ranking. CA BOE Prop. 19 · BOE LTA 2025/009
R2 Rental paths need a written market rent
Hold-as-rental is ranked only when an independent written market-rent estimate for Property A (comparable rentals, adjusted for condition) exists. Until then it is a floor. Rental use does not qualify for the Prop. 19 family-home exclusion, so the rental path is modeled with full reassessment.		 Independent rent estimate on file. Rental path shown as a floor with the break-even net rent it would need to match the sell path. CA BOE Prop. 19 · IRS Pub. 527
R3 Sell-now versus staged investing (Black Swan test)
Choose staged investing (3M–2Y Treasuries for 24–36 months, then phase into equities) over an immediate lump-sum equity investment if BOTH hold: (i) in the base case the 10-year real wealth difference (staged minus lump) is no worse than −10% of net proceeds; and (ii) in the crash test (a 2008-type or 1929-type equity fall beginning at the transfer date) the 10-year real difference (staged minus lump) is at least +5% of net proceeds. Choose lump-sum if the base-case advantage of lump-sum over staged is at least 5% of net proceeds. Otherwise record "no clear winner" and decide on liquidity and owner tolerance, documented in writing.		 Base case: staged ≥ lump − 10% of proceeds. Crash: staged − lump ≥ +5% of proceeds. Lump-sum preferred when lump − staged ≥ 5% of proceeds in base case. No clear winner; decide on documented liquidity needs. U.S. Treasury rates · NBER cycle dates
R4 Sell versus living: break-even imputed rent
Sell is preferred over living in Property A when the monthly imputed rent the heir could earn elsewhere (what-if input) is below the break-even rent computed by the model over 10 years, AND an independent market-rent estimate confirms that the heir's avoided housing cost is at or above that break-even. If no rent evidence exists, sell-versus-live is "not yet decidable".		 Independent rent evidence on file; comparison at the 10-year horizon in the base case. Not yet decidable. CA BOE Prop. 19 · IRS Topic 701 (home sale exclusion)
R5 Mixed path needs a clear §121 and Prop. 19 benefit
Live 2 years then sell, then stage, is preferred to a pure sell only if the two-year occupancy adds at least 3% of net proceeds to 10-year real wealth in the base case AND Prop. 19 status is verified. If Prop. 19 is not verified, the mixed path is shown but not preferred.		 ≥ +3% of proceeds, base case, 10-year horizon. Pure sell path stands; mixed path shown for information. IRS Topic 701 · CA BOE Prop. 19
R6 Estate-level exposure overrides path ranking
If the projected gross estate in the transfer year exceeds 90% of the federal basic exclusion amount for that year (2027 published: $15,480,000; later years indexed), the estate liquidity plan, the portability election and gift-history review are completed first. The path ranking is then secondary.		 Gross estate > 90% of the indexed basic exclusion in the transfer year. Estate tasks first. IRS IR-2026-133 · IRS estate-tax FAQ
R7 High entry-risk flag
Any path whose 5-year real loss in a 2008-type crash starting at transfer exceeds 40% of net proceeds is labelled "high entry risk". The flag does not change the ranking by itself but requires written owner sign-off before implementation.		 5-year real loss > 40% of net proceeds. Owner sign-off required. NBER cycle dates · Black Swan staging test
R8 Law changes count only after enactment
A proposed or pending law changes a ranking only after it is enacted and effective. Proposals appear as sensitivity cases, not in the base case. Status is re-checked at least annually and before any irreversible step.		 Enacted and effective. Base case unchanged; sensitivity shown. Official enacted-vs-proposed monitor
R9 Rerun and record
After every verified input change the model is rebuilt and the site regenerated. If the recommendation changes, the decision log records the date, the input that changed, and the reason. The recommendation is never changed by editing an assumption to fit a preferred outcome; assumption changes are logged separately from fact changes.		 Dated log entry for every change. Change is not accepted. Decision-log template
