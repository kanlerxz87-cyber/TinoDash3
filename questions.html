Decision-readiness register

Questions first. Answers second. Decision last.

This is the full public list of what has to be known before a reasonable choice exists: the question, the honest current answer, why it changes the outcome, and exactly how the missing information gets discovered. A blank is a gate, not an invitation to substitute a round number.

Privacy instruction

Answers go in the private room.

Collect documents in an encrypted household and counsel workspace. Never enter a name, address, parcel number, account balance or lease into this site, its repository, an issue tracker or a form.

Contain prior exposure

Do not solve a privacy problem with a better public dossier.

Earlier drafts of this project carried case-specific identifiers into a public repository. Deleting them from the current pages does not remove them from earlier commits, caches or copies. The containment steps and the remaining history risk are recorded on the privacy page and in the coordination board.

  • Do not paste names, parcels, bills, ledgers or balances into Git, issues, screenshots or forms.
  • Rewrite history only with explicit owner authorisation and a coordinated force-push.
  • Log every share of a document outside the encrypted workspace.
  • Use Property A / Property B labels in every working draft.

Rebuild rule

Any validated input change forces a rebuild.

After valuation, rate area, rent, insurance, debt, capital, occupancy or law-status changes, re-run the comparison and record whether the leader moved. Do not preserve an earlier conclusion because it was convenient.

Order of operations

Occupancy before tax, tax before investment.

The occupancy decision determines the property-tax outcome. The property-tax outcome changes the carry cost. The carry cost changes how much of the proceeds can be invested and when. Doing these in a different order means doing them twice.
