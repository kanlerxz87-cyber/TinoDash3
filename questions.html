Contain prior exposure

Do not solve a privacy problem by making a better public dossier.

Do not paste names, addresses, parcel numbers, trust pages, tax bills, rental ledgers, account balances, identity documents, appraisal reports, or solar/utility account identifiers into Git, issues, screenshots, public maps, or this site.

  • Remove any legacy public copies after counsel assesses retention obligations.
  • Use access-limited document storage and a sharing log.
  • Redact before any professional receives a nonessential copy.
  • Use Property A / Property B labels in working drafts until private counsel needs identifiers.
