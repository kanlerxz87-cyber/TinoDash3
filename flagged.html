Flagged for Review

Open items do not block use of the framework. They do block treating any projected winner as a recommendation.

Highest urgency

Prior public-data exposure

Git history should be reviewed for names, addresses, parcel IDs and exact records. This repository began with only a README, and this build stores none. If older remote history exists elsewhere, use a privacy/security specialist; history rewriting requires coordinated force-push and does not erase forks or caches.

Unverified

Property A tax-rate area

Lookup requires a private parcel identifier. Do not place it in the repository. Owner or adviser should retrieve the official rate privately from the county portal and enter only a rounded rate into private workpapers.

Unverified

Insurance and market rent

No specific premium or rent can be verified from an official public authority. Obtain at least two written quotes and independent rent opinions for each property.

Unverified

Values, basis, leases, occupancy

Current values are private estimates. Obtain a qualified date-of-death appraisal, title/debt records, lease review, and written occupancy plan.

Model limitation

Taxes and cash flows

The interactive chart intentionally omits transaction-specific tax calculations until basis, filing status, depreciation, sale date, debt and costs are verified. Net proceeds is an explicit user scenario.
