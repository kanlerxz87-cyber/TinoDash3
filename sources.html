Line-by-line source discipline

Official links, source dates, and a clear line between law and proposal.

Every legal, tax, fee, regulatory, and rate statement published on this site is linked to an official authority. Property-specific facts stay blank unless privately verified. A proposal is never treated as enacted merely because it could matter.

Source refresh rule

Conditional Prop. H model — terms only

Keep Property B’s potential local parcel-tax exposure separate and conditional.

The official 2026 ballot pamphlet describes Proposition H as a proposed annual parcel tax for public transit operations from 1 Jul 2027 through 30 Jun 2042, adjusted annually for inflation. The official results page currently reports a preliminary Yes result, but certification and operative implementation must still be verified; it is not treated as enacted law here. It is not a Property A transfer cost and it does not change inherited basis.

Periodic review procedure

Assign a named reviewer in the private decision file.

  • Federal: IRS estate/gift updates, Form 706 instructions, IRS publications at event/sale year.
  • California: BOE Prop. 13/19 materials, FTB return instructions and annual forms.
  • County: assessor tax-rate area/bill, recorder transfer/recording fee schedules.
  • Local: rental authority, election result, enacted ordinances, proposed legislation / effective dates.
  • Record URL, publication date, access date, status, reviewer, and model effect after each refresh.

Verification limits

What was intentionally left blank

No official authority can verify an unnamed household’s actual property rate area, assessment, fair market value, rents, costs, debt, insurance quote, lease status, trust terms, estate size, prior gifts, or occupancy intent from this public site. Those rows are blank—not guessed.

Irregularity to review

Public source data can expose a household even without a name.

Combining a postal area, exact historic tax trail, exact value, rental amount, unique solar pattern, or unit configuration may make a property searchable. The site deliberately uses broad generic examples and formulas instead. If any future draft becomes more specific, run the privacy scan and counsel review before publishing.
