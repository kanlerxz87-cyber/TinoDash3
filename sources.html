Line-by-line source discipline

Every claim links to the authority that decides it.

A government link is not, by itself, verification. Each row below records what the source actually says, the date or limit it applies to, and what would make it wrong. Anything that could not be confirmed from an official page is left blank and flagged rather than filled in.

Refresh rule

Recheck before action.

Snapshot reviewed 25 November 2026. Recheck at least annually, at the inheritance event, before any filing, before listing, before signing a lease and before changing the investment policy.

Irregularity to keep visible

Removing a name is not anonymisation.

A postal area plus an exact historic assessment trail, an exact valuation, a unit count and a solar pattern can recombine into identification even with every name removed. That is why this site publishes rates, formulas and multiples instead of figures.

Review procedure

Assign a named reviewer in the private file.

  • Federal: IRS estate and gift updates, Form 706 and Pub. 523 at the event year and sale year.
  • California: BOE Prop. 13 and Prop. 19 material, FTB instructions for the sale year.
  • County: assessor rate area and bill, recorder fee and transfer-tax schedules.
  • Local: election results, ordinances, and any proposed change with a file number.
  • Record URL, publication date, access date, status, reviewer and model effect.
