This page is the shared “needs review” register. It separates a missing fact from a judgment call, gives it an owner and source route, and prevents a clean-looking dashboard from implying a false conclusion.

Decision discipline

Unresolved is an acceptable answer.

Do not close a flag by inserting a plausible round number. Close it only with dated evidence, a source, and a documented decision on how the evidence changes the model.

Privacy control policy

Publication rules for every future improvement

  • Use only Property A / Property B labels on public pages.
  • Do not publish direct identifiers or combinations that make a property readily searchable.
  • Use formulas, rounded generic examples, and normalized units for public explanations.
  • Keep private documents outside Git, use access-limited storage, and log sharing.
  • Run a content scan before every deployment and have a human review any new table, chart, image, filename, alt text, URL parameter, or metadata.
Public-data containment rule

Removing a name is not enough.

Unique dates, values, assessment trails, unit counts, rental figures, solar patterns, and location clues can recombine into identification. When in doubt, remove the detail from the public artifact and keep it in the private evidence room.
