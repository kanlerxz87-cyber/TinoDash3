Attorney & CPA Briefing Sheet

Purpose: prepare for a possible trust inheritance of Property A around 2040–2050. The household already owns mixed-use Property B and can occupy part while retaining rental income. Goal: compare live, rent, sell/invest and mixed paths at the same transfer date and horizon.

Facts to confirm

  • Trust/title, beneficiaries, debts, liquidity
  • Gross estate, prior taxable gifts, portability
  • Date-of-death appraisal and income-tax basis
  • Property A assessor value, tax-rate area
  • Property B leases, basis and depreciation
  • Long-term occupancy intent and evidence

Advice requested

  • Separate estate tax, basis and assessment
  • Same-tax-year sale estimate first
  • Prop 19 eligibility/allowance and deadlines
  • Federal/CA gain and depreciation recapture
  • Home-sale exclusion on live-then-sell
  • §1031 desirability before any sale contract
