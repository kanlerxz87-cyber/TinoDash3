What would change the answer?

Property tax

Long-term occupancy

If the heir will not use Property A as a principal residence long term, do not build the plan around a Proposition 19 parent-child exclusion.

Evidence gap

Market rent

A signed independent rent opinion could move renting above selling. Until obtained, rent results are scenario inputs—not findings.

Tax

Basis and sale timing

A qualified date-of-death valuation and a same-tax-year sale estimate determine taxable gain; do not substitute today’s estimate.

Portfolio risk

Concentration and crash entry

Two Bay Area properties increase geographic concentration; selling reduces it, but immediate stock entry creates sequence risk.

Pre-commit before figures arrive

Live: choose only if long-term occupancy, lifestyle fit, and exclusion eligibility are documented. Rent: choose only if verified net yield exceeds the agreed hurdle after vacancy and capital costs. Sell: choose if after-tax proceeds and diversification dominate verified housing utility. Mixed: choose when liquidity and optionality are worth accepting partial exposure.

Deadline-driven inheritance timeline

  1. Now / annual review
    Inventory privately; ask counsel about trust terms, portability, prior taxable gifts, estate liquidity, and current/proposed law. Refresh written decision rules.
  2. Before transfer
    Obtain independent Property A value and market-rent range; inspect both buildings; create five-year capital plans; decide whether replacement real estate is even desired.
  3. Date of death
    Secure qualified written valuation and records. Keep estate tax, income-tax basis, and property-tax assessment as three separate workstreams.
  4. Promptly after transfer
    Counsel confirms filing deadlines and title; assessor confirms current Proposition 19 forms, occupancy tests and applicable taxable-value calculation.
  5. Before sale
    Model same-tax-year sale first. Tax adviser estimates federal/California gain, depreciation/recapture, transfer tax, recording, debt payoff and selling costs. Ask exchange counsel before signing if §1031 is under consideration.
  6. Before proceeds arrive
    Sign an investment policy: liquidity reserve, tax reserve, allocation, Treasury staging, rebalance rules, and prohibited actions.
  7. Every year
    Update official law, rents, insurance, maintenance, taxes, portfolio assumptions and extreme scenarios; record decision, date, owner and why.

Decision questions and missing information

Legal & estate

  • Who legally receives each asset, and when?
  • Projected gross estate and includible prior gifts?
  • Was portability elected in any prior estate?
  • Debts, liens, trust restrictions, co-beneficiaries?

Discover: private counsel-led inventory and document review.

Tax

  • Qualified date-of-death basis?
  • Property A tax-rate area and published rate?
  • Prop 19 occupancy eligibility and timing?
  • Depreciation history for Property B?

Discover: assessor written response and CPA workpapers.

Property operations

  • Independent sale and rent ranges?
  • Five-year roof, seismic, systems plan?
  • Insurable limits and written quotes?
  • Verified leases and local rent rules?

Discover: appraiser, broker, inspector, insurer and local counsel.

Household & portfolio

  • Where will household live and for how long?
  • Liquidity floor and spending needs?
  • Maximum property/stock drawdown tolerated?
  • Who manages tenants or investments?

Discover: signed household policy before projections.
