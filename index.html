Executive Summary

Anonymized inheritance decision framework for Property A (Cupertino, 95014) while already owning Property B (San Francisco 3-unit, 94122). All figures rounded to nearest $10.

What Would Change the Answer (First-Screen Triggers):
  • Prop 19 Occupancy: If the heir will NOT live in Property A for 1+ years, Property A reassesses to full market value (~$3.1M+ → ~$41,400/yr tax), making Sell & Invest or 1031 Exchange strongly favored.
  • Federal Estate Exclusion: Currently $15,000,000 (2026). If total estate (Property A + liquid assets) exceeds the future exclusion at 2040–2050, liquidity planning & portability (Form 706) become critical.
  • Equity vs. Rent Spread: If net rental yield on Property A (<2.5% after reassessment) trails diversified equity returns (~7%), selling at inheritance with a full IRC §1014 step-up in basis maximizes after-tax wealth.
