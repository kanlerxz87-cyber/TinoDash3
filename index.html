First screen: what would change the answer

The five facts most likely to move the decision

Agree these decision gates in writing before receiving estimates. That prevents a later model from being reverse-engineered to defend a preferred outcome.

Decision blocked
  1. Date-of-death fair market value and estate basisSame-tax-year sale analysis cannot begin until an independent written valuation establishes the inherited basis and any applicable estate election is known.
  2. Actual occupancy commitmentProperty-tax relief is not assumed. A long-term primary-residence decision must satisfy the applicable California occupancy, exemption, and filing requirements.
  3. Property A tax-rate area and transfer costsA postal area is not a tax-rate area. Obtain the private tax bill / assessor record and verify the municipality-specific transfer-tax treatment before pricing a sale.
  4. Market rent, condition, leases, and five-year capital planWithout independent rent and condition evidence, rental paths are only floors or placeholders—not an evidence-based return estimate.
  5. Whole-estate liquidity, prior gifts, and portabilityThe federal threshold applies to the whole taxable estate and prior taxable gifts, not one home. Cash needs and portability must be reviewed with the executor, attorney, and CPA.

Decision options

Compare decisions, not slogans

Every option should be compared at the same Year 0 transfer date and the same 5-, 10-, 25-, or 50-year horizon. Unknown debt, capital work, insurance, and actual taxes are deliberately not disguised as precise estimates.

Sale path

Sell in Year 0

Test a same-tax-year sale against verified date-of-death basis, sale costs, and whole-estate liquidity needs. Do not use a prior owner’s old purchase price as a substitute for inherited basis.

Current-law map

Keep three tax systems separate

A property-tax assessment, income-tax basis, and estate-tax measurement answer different questions. Combining them is a common model error.

A deliberate limitation

There is no “best plan” button yet.

A projection with unverified basis, rent, debt, capital work, eligibility, and tax facts would create false precision. The dashboard will show which missing item changes the answer and who should supply it.
