## Model rerun and decision interpretation

Ran the pure engine (`assets/engine.js`) at the 10-year horizon using its default normalized assumptions, with taxes off and no household data. The leading path differs by designed scenario: rent-then-sell in the irregular baseline; staged sale proceeds in the 1929-style and 2008-style shocks; and rent-then-sell in the stagflation sequence. This is a scenario ranking, not an empirical probability, forecast, or personalized recommendation. The scenario-dependent ranking is itself evidence that there is no single robust recommendation from the available public inputs. T-028 changed no engine assumptions or household inputs.
